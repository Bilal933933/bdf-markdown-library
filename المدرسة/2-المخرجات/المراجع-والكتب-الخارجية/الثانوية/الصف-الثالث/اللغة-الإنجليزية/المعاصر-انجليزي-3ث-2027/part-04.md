WRITING SKILL: ARGUMENTATIVE ESSAY
• What is an Argumentative Essay?
- An argumentative essay is a type of writing where you give your claim on a topic and support it with reasons and evidence. You also mention the opposite claim and explain why you think your claim is stronger.
المقال الجدلي هو نوع من الكتابة تعبر فيه عن رأيك في موضوع معين، وتدعمه بالأسباب والأدلة، وأيضا تعرض الرأي المعارض موضحا لماذا رأيك أقوى.

Structure and Key Components البناء والمكونات الرئيسية
1 Introduction: (المقدمة)
• Introduce the topic.
• Give background.
• State your claim (thesis).
في المقدمة، عرف الموضوع اعطى خلفية للموضوع واذكر رأيك.

2 Body Paragraphs: (فقرات المتن/الموضوع)
a. Supporting Paragraphs (فقرات تدعم الرأي)
• Present at least two reasons for your claim.
• Use examples or evidence.
اكتب فقرتين أو أكثر تدعم رأيك باستخدام الأسباب والأمثلة.
b. Opposing View (الرأي المعارض)
• Mention the opposite side - say why you disagree.
اعرض الرأي الآخر ثم قم برفضه بأدلة توضح ضعفه.

3 Conclusion: (الخاتمة)
• Restate your claim.
• Summarize your main points.
اختم المقال بإعادة صياغة رأيك وتلخيص النقاط الرئيسية.

Useful Expressions تعبيرات مفيدة
Stating your claim: (للإبداء الرأي)
• I believe that...
• I think it is clear that...
• In my opinion...

Supporting your claim: (للدعم الرأي)
• Firstly,...
• For example,...
• Another reason is...

Introducing the other side: (لعرض الرأي الآخر)
• Some people say that...
• On the other hand,...
• It is argued that...

Argumentative essay terminology (مصطلحات المقال الجدلي)

Term | Definition
--- | ---
Essay | A piece of writing about one topic. It has an introduction, body, and conclusion. مقالة تتناول موضوعا محددا وتتكون من مقدمة وجسم وخاتمة.
Argumentative essay | An essay that gives an opinion and supports it with reasons. مقالة تقدم رأيا وتدعمه بالأسباب والأدلة.
Opinion | What you believe or think. الرأي الشخصي أو ما تعتقده.
Reason | Why you think your opinion is right. السبب الذي يجعلك ترى أن رأيك صحيح.
Evidence | A fact or example that supports your reason. دليل مثل حقيقة أو مثال يدعم السبب.
Introduction | The beginning of the essay that tells the topic and your opinion. مقدمة المقال وتعرض الموضوع ورأيك.
Body paragraph | A paragraph in the middle that gives reasons and examples. فقرة في وسط المقال تقدم الأسباب والأمثلة.
Supporting paragraph | A body paragraph that explains your opinion. فقرة تدعم رأيك وتوضح الأسباب.
Opposing view | The other side of the argument. الرأي المعارض أو الجانب الآخر من النقاش.
Refutation | Saying why the opposing view is weak or wrong. دحض الرأي الآخر وتوضيح ضعفه أو خطئه.
Conclusion | The last part that restates your opinion and summarizes the main points. الخاتمة التي تعيد صياغة رأيك وتلخص النقاط الأساسية.
Transition word | A word that connects ideas (e.g., however, firstly). عبارات انتقالية تربط بين الأفكار مثل (أولا، لكن، لذلك).
Topic sentence | The main idea of a paragraph. الجملة الأساسية التي توضح فكرة الفقرة.
Thesis statement | A sentence in the introduction that shows your main opinion. جملة في المقدمة توضح الرأي الأساسي للمقال.
Hook | An interesting sentence at the start to catch the reader's attention. جملة جذابة في البداية لجذب انتباه القارئ.
Clarify | To make your ideas easy to understand. توضيح الأفكار لتكون سهلة الفهم.

An Argumentative Essay

1. An argumentative essay is a type of writing in which you give your claim about a topic.
2. The writer supports his/her claim with reasons and evidence.
3. The writer also mentions the opposing view and explains why he/she disagrees with it.
4. The structure of an argumentative essay consists of three main parts: an introduction, body paragraph and a conclusion.
5. In the introduction, we introduce the topic, give background information and state the thesis statement.
6. A thesis statement is a sentence in the introduction that shows the writer's main opinion.
7. A hook is an interesting opening sentence used to catch the reader's attention.
8. The body usually contains supporting paragraphs and a paragraph presenting the opposing view.
9. In the supporting paragraphs, we give at least two reasons for our claim.
10. We use examples or evidence to support and clarify our reasons.
11. An opposing view is the other side of the argument.
12. A refutation explains why the opposing view is weak or wrong.
13. To state an opinion, we can use "I believe that ...," "I think it is clear that ..." or "In my opinion...".
14. To support an opinion, we can use "Firstly ...," "For example ..." or "Another reason is ...".
15. To introduce the other side, we can use "Some people say that ...," "On the other hand..." or "It is argued that ...".
16. To refute the other side, we can use "However, I disagree because ...," "This may be true, but ..." or "I believe this is not strong enough because ...".
17. In the conclusion, we restate our claim and summarize the main points.
18. To conclude an argumentative essay, we can use "In conclusion...," "To sum up ..." or "That's why I believe ...".
19. A topic sentence presents the main idea of a paragraph.
20. We use transition words, such as firstly and however, to connect ideas clearly.

Essay | A piece of writing about one topic. It has an introduction, body, and conclusion. (مقالة تتناول موضوعا محددا وتتكون من مقدمة وجسم وخاتمة.)

Body paragraph | A paragraph in the middle that gives reasons and examples. (فقرة في وسط المقال تقدم الأسباب والأمثلة.)
Supporting paragraph | A body paragraph that explains your opinion. (فقرة تدعم رأيك وتوضح الأسباب.)

Thankfully, there are steps you can take to reduce your risk of a heart attack. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle is key. This includes eating a balanced diet low in saturated fats, exercising regularly, managing stress, and maintaining a healthy weight. Climate change must be seen not only as an environmental crisis but also as a profound issue of global justice. While industrialized nations are largely responsible for the majority of greenhouse gas emissions, it is often low-income and marginalized communities - particularly in the Global South - that bear the brunt of its consequences.
These populations might face rising sea levels, long droughts, food shortage, and extreme weather events that threaten their health, and safety.
Climate justice emphasizes that we cannot ignore these effects. It calls for fair policies, shared decisions, and cooperation to fix the unequal burdens of climate change. People who cause the least damage must not suffer the most.
Youth around the world are actively working to stop climate change, showing great leadership and creativity. At its core, climate justice is about equity, responsibility, and giving a voice to those who are often unheard in global debates. Without justice, there can be no true solution to the climate crisis.
