WRITING SKILL : REVIEW WRITING

• What is a Review? ما هو المقال النقدي؟
- A review is a written opinion about something you have experienced, such as a movie, book, restaurant, product, or place. It is both informative and persuasive.
المقال النقدى هو تقييم مكتوب لشيء قمت بتجربته، مثل فيلم، كتاب، مطعم، منتج أو مكان. وهو يجمع بين المعلومات والإقناع.

• Purpose of a Review غرض العرض النقدى
1. To Inform لإعطاء المعلومة
- Describe what the item is like: its features, qualities, or atmosphere.
2. To Persuade لإقناع القارئ
- Encourage or discourage the audience to try, buy, or visit it.

Structure of a Review بنية العرض النقدى
1. Introduction & Overall Impression المقدمة والانطباع العام
- Introduce the subject (movie, book, restaurant, product, place). تقديم الموضوع (فيلم، كتاب، مطعم، منتج، مكان).
- Provide basic information: title, author/director, location, type. تقديم معلومات أساسية: العنوان، المؤلف/المخرج، الموقع، النوع.
- State your overall impression briefly. ذكر الانطباع العام بإيجاز.
2. Specific Details تفاصيل محددة
- Describe main aspects (e.g., plot, characters, acting, atmosphere, design, performance). وصف الجوانب الرئيسية (مثل الحبكة، الشخصيات، التمثيل، الجو، التصميم، الأداء).
- Use descriptive adjectives. استخدام الصفات الوصفية.
3. Pros and Cons الإيجابيات والسلبيات
- Explain what you liked and didn't like. شرح ما أعجبك وما لم يعجبك.
- Give reasons and support with examples. تقديم الأسباب مدعومة بأمثلة.
4. Conclusion & Recommendation الخاتمة والتوصية
- Restate your overall opinion. إعادة صياغة الرأى العام.
- Recommend or not recommend, and say for whom it is suitable. التوصية بالثئ أو عدم التوصية، وذكر الفئة المناسبة.

Review Writing Tips إرشادات لكتابة العرض النقدى
- Be clear and concise. كن واضحاً ومختصراً.
- Balance fact with opinion. وازن بين الحقائق والرأى الشخصى.
- Use varied vocabulary and adjectives. استخدم مفردات وصفية متنوعة.
- Keep a logical flow from introduction to conclusion. حافظ على تسلسل منطقى من المقدمة إلى الخاتمة.

Review Writing

1. A review is a written opinion about something the writer has experienced.
2. A review may be written about a movie, book, restaurant, product or place.
3. A review should be both informative and persuasive.
4. One purpose of a review is to inform the reader about the reviewed item.
5. To inform means to provide facts or details.
6. A review describes the item's features, qualities or atmosphere.
7. Another purpose of a review is to persuade the reader.
8. A review may encourage or discourage the audience from trying, buying or visiting something.
9. The main parts of a review are the introduction, specific details, pros and cons, and the conclusion.
10. In the introduction, the writer introduces the subject of the review.
11. The introduction provides basic information such as the title, author or director, location and type.
12. The writer should briefly state his or her overall impression in the introduction.
13. An overall impression is the writer's general feeling or opinion.
14. In the specific-details section, the writer describes the main aspects of the reviewed item.
15. A film review may describe the plot, characters, acting, atmosphere and performance.
16. The writer should use descriptive adjectives to describe qualities clearly.
17. The pros are the advantages or positive points of the reviewed item.
18. The cons are its disadvantages or negative points.
19. The writer should explain what he or she liked and did not like.
20. Opinions should be supported with reasons and examples.
21. In the conclusion, the writer restates the overall opinion.
22. The writer should recommend or not recommend the item and explain who it is suitable for.
23. A recommendation is advice about whether someone should try something.
24. A feature is a distinctive quality or characteristic.
25. The atmosphere is the feeling or mood of a place.
26. An opinion is a personal view or judgement.
27. A good review should be clear and concise.
28. The writer should balance facts with personal opinions.
29. The writer should use varied vocabulary and descriptive adjectives.
30. A review should have a logical flow from the introduction to the conclusion.
