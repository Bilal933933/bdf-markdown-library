٣ تتكون جملة الأمر / النهي في الكلام غير المباشر من:
المصدر .inf + to / not to + مخاطب + فعل إبلاغ + مبلغ
Mom asked Leen to open the door.
Father warned Amr not to eat too much.

٤ يستخدم فعل إبلاغ مناسب ويستخدم بعده المخاطب (اسم أو ضمير مفعول):
told قال - asked طلب - commanded أمر - reminded ذكر - warned حذر - helped ساعد - wanted أراد - invited دعي - instructed أمر - encouraged شجع - advised نصح - taught علم - expected توقع - threatened هدد + obj. المخاطب ......
- I told Sama to help her mother.
- Grandpa advised me to work hard at school.

٥ لا تستخدم علامات التنصيص ("......") في الأمر أو النهي في صيغة غير المباشر:
- "Never play with matches", said my mother. (مباشر)
- My mother warned me not to play with matches. (غير مباشر)

٦ تستخدم (to + inf.) بمعنى (أن ...) في الإثبات:
- I told him to do more exercise.

٧ تستخدم (not to + inf.) بمعنى (ألا ...) في النفي:
- The officer ordered the soldiers not to leave their places.

٨ تتحول الضمائر حسب المتكلم والمخاطب (راجع تحويلات الضمائر بهذه الوحدة):
- Amira said, "Lend me your camera, Ali". (مباشر)
- Amira asked Ali to lend her his camera. (غير مباشر)

٩ تتحول بعض ظروف الزمان والمكان (راجع تحويلات الظروف بهذه الوحدة):
- "Bring this book with you tomorrow", said Miss Mona. (مباشر)

Sentence / Answer / Explanation

1. Ali .......... that he had taken my mobile by mistake.
a. told
b. informed
c. convinced
d. admitted
Answer: admitted
Explanation: عدم وجود مفعول جعل المعنى الصحيح admitted.

2. "I bought this tablet yesterday," Noura said. Noura said that she had bought .......... .
a. this tablet yesterday
b. these tablets then
c. that tablet tomorrow
d. that tablet the day before
Answer: that tablet the day before
Explanation: تتحول yesterday إلى the day before و this إلى that ويتحول الماضي البسيط إلى ماضي تام. this → that, yesterday → the day before.

3. Mazen .......... me why he couldn't attend the meeting.
a. told
b. said
c. asked
d. wondered
Answer: told
Explanation: Mazen told me why. الجملة هنا جملة خبرية وليست استفهامية لذا الحل الصحيح told.

4. Yara .......... she would make a cake for my birthday party.
a. says
b. told
c. tells
d. said
Answer: said
Explanation: عدم وجود مفعول في الجملة وزمن الجملة المنقولة ماضي جعل الحل الصحيح said.

5. "These books are mine," Ahmed said. Ahmed said that .......... books were .......... .
a. these/mine
b. this/his
c. those/mine
d. those/his
Answer: those / his
Explanation: وجود الفعل said جعل ضرورة استخدام those مع تحويل ضمير الملكية mine إلى his.

6. Mr Gamal said that water .......... at 100°C.
a. had boiled
b. boils
c. was boiling
d. would boil
Answer: boils
Explanation: لا نغير الزمن عند نقل حقيقة علمية ثابتة أو حقيقة مطلقة. universal fact → present simple remains.

7. A moment ago, Ahmed said that he .......... some clothes tomorrow.
a. would buy
b. had bought
c. will buy
d. bought
Answer: will buy
Explanation: عند نقل الكلام بعد وقت قصير جدا، يمكن إبقاء الزمن وظرف الزمان دون تغيير إذا كان المعنى الزمني مازال صحيحًا. very recent report → tense may remain unchanged.

8. Yara said that she wished she .......... in Paris.
a. had lived
b. would live
c. lives
d. lived
Answer: lived
Explanation: It is time و wish يبقى الماضي البسيط بعد rather دون تحويل إلى الماضي التام. wish + past simple → remains past simple.

9. Sama said, "I will do the shopping. I will clean the kitchen." Sama said that she would do the shopping .......... she would clean the kitchen.
a. but
b. and that
c. because
d. unless
Answer: and that
Explanation: عند نقل جملتين متتاليتين للمتحدث نفسه نربط بينهما باستخدام and that أو and add that. statement 1 + and that + statement 2.

10. My mother warned me .......... out alone.
a. don't go
b. to not going
c. not to go
d. not going
Answer: not to go
Explanation: في الأمر المنفي نستخدم فعل الإبلاغ ثم المفعول به ثم المصدر. reporting verb + object + not to + infinitive.

At home, we depend on household appliances like washing machines, microwaves, and vacuum cleaners. These devices save time and make chores easier. A recent study reported that most people use over five machines every single day. Even our free time is shaped by technology — smartphones, laptops, and smart TVs that keep us entertained and connected with friends and family.

In the workplace, machines help us work faster and more accurately. Office workers use computers to write emails, prepare documents, and join online meetings. In factories, robots build cars, pack products, and carry heavy items. One factory manager said, “Our production has doubled since we started using automated systems.”

Our health is also monitored by machines. Smartwatches track our heart rate, steps, and sleep quality. Doctors use advanced scanners and machines to detect illnesses early and plan treatments.

However, some people worry that we rely too much on machines. They fear that if technology fails, our lives could come to a standstill. As one teacher explained, “Students struggle to write without spellcheck or grammar tools.”

While machines bring comfort, speed, and efficiency, it’s important to remember that we should control them — not the other way around. Life with machines is easier, but we must stay in charge of our choices and skills. After all, technology should serve us, not rule us.
