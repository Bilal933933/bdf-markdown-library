Coastal cities(1) such as Alexandria face great risks(2) from climate change. As global(3) temperatures rise(4), glaciers(5) and ice sheets melt, and sea levels increase. Scientists emphasize(6) that even a small rise in sea level can cause serious flooding in low-lying areas(7) that can stop business for days. This must be one of the greatest threats to homes, schools, and hospitals. Experts warn that the damage might be worse than many people think, and floods could be happening more often in the near future.
In Alexandria, many people live close to the sea. They notice that the waves are stronger and sometimes reach the streets. Families worry about their homes and shop owners worry about their shops, but they also take precautions(8) to stay safe. Fishermen explain that the sea is changing.
They say the waves must have been caused by global warming, since storms can’t have become stronger for no reason. They flooded already.
Still, people remain hopeful(9) and try to prepare in advance(10). The state is also working to reduce the dangers. It builds sea walls to protect neighborhoods and invests(11) in renewable energy to reduce pollution. Authorities(12) run awareness campaigns(13) to teach citizens how to act during storms. Officials admit that without these measures, the losses might have been far greater. With these efforts, the risks may be reduced, and Alexandria can remain safe for future generations.
Many experts also argue that we shouldn’t have ignored the problem of climate change. Citizens should have taken more responsibility for reducing pollution in their daily lives.
