Divide: 736 ÷ 4

Multiplication can help check the answer.

Known that
Multiplication and division by the same number are opposite operations or inverse operations. One operation undoes the other.

Learn 1 Order of operations
Find : 6 ÷ 2 ÷ 2
• Sara solved the problem by subtracting first and then dividing.
What did she get?
• Bassem solved the problem by dividing first and then subtracting.
What did he get?

Sara
Think: 6 - 2 = 4
6 - 2 ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2 = 2

Bassem
Think: 2 ÷ 2 = 1
6 - 2 ÷ 2 = 6 - 1 = 5

There seem to be two correct answers.
When solving problems with more than one operation, you need to know which operation to do first. A special set of rules, called the order of operations, can be used to solve problems with more than one operation.

Order of Operations
First, perform any operations in parentheses.
Next, multiply and divide from left to right.
Then, add and subtract from left to right.

So, the correct answer to 6 - 2 ÷ 2 is 5 and Bassem solved the problem in a correct way.

Example 1
Follow the order of operations to find the value of each expression.
a. (8 - 2) x 6 = ____
b. 5 + 10 ÷ 5 = ____
c. 3 x (4 + 6) = ____
d. 6 - 4 ÷ 2 = ____
e. (5 + 3) ÷ 2 = ____
f. 14 ÷ 7 x 2 = ____

Solution
a. (8 - 2) x 6 = 6 x 6 = 36. Do what is in the parentheses first, next multiply.
b. 5 + 10 ÷ 5 = 5 + 2 = 7. There are no parentheses, so divide first. Then add.
c. 3 x (4 + 6) = 3 x 10 = 30. Do what is in the parentheses first, next multiply.
d. 6 - 4 ÷ 2 = 6 - 2 = 4. There are no parentheses, so divide first. Then subtract.
e. (5 + 3) ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Do what is in the parentheses first, next divide.
f. 14 ÷ 7 x 2 = 2 x 2 = 4. There are no parentheses, so divide and multiply from left to right.

Example 2
Follow the order of operations to find the result.
a. 15 + 24 ÷ 8 - 2 = ____
b. 15 + 24 ÷ (8 - 2) = ____

Solution
a. 15 + 24 ÷ 8 - 2 = 15 + 3 - 2 = 18 - 2 = 16. There are no parentheses, so divide. Then add and subtract from left to right.
b. 15 + 24 ÷ (8 - 2) = 15 + 24 ÷ 6 = 15 + 4 = 19. Do what is in the parentheses first, next divide. Then add.
