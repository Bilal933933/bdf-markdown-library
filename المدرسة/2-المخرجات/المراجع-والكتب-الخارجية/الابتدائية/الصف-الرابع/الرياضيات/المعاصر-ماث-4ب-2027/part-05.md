Another Way to find area

Complete the figure as a big rectangle A and a small rectangle B.

Area of the rectangle A
A = l × w

Learn

During Emad’s visit to the Zoo, he read this information.

Can you help him to calculate the tall of the giraffe?

The giraffe in the zoo is 3 times as tall as the kangaroo. The kangaroo is 2 m tall. How tall is the giraffe?

Read and Understand

What do you know?
• The kangaroo is 2 m tall.
• The giraffe is 3 time as tall as the kangaroo

What are you trying to find?
Find how tall is the giraffe.

Plan and Solve

What strategy will you use?
Strategy: Write a multiplicative comparison equation

1. Use a letter to represent the unknown.
Let the tall of the giraffe be x.

2. The giraffe is 3 times as tall as the kangaroo means, x is 3 times 2

Kangaroo's tall: 2 m
Giraffe's tall: 2 m | 2 m | 2 m
Comparison bar model: x

3. Write an equation: x = 3 × 2

4. Solve the equation: x = 6

So, the giraffe is 6 m tall.

Notes for parents:
• Common Error: Your child may incorrectly place the unknown in an equation. For example, if your child is asked to write 12 is 3 times the number a, he/she may write 12 × 3 = a, instead of 12 = 3 × a or 3 × a = 12
